TENDER NOTICE REF NO: RWSS/ZONE-IV/PUMP-PROC/2026/088
DEPARTMENT: Rural Water Supply and Sanitation Engineering Department (Fictional)
SUBJECT: Supply, Delivery, and Site Testing of 7.5 kW (10 HP) Openwell Submersible Pumpsets for Community Water Supply Schemes.

SECTION 1: SCOPE OF SUPPLY
1.1 The contractor shall manufacture, inspect, test, pack, and deliver 150 units of Openwell Submersible Pumpsets suitable for direct submerged operation in village open intake wells and masonry community sumps.
1.2 The equipment shall comprise a multi-stage centrifugal pump directly coupled to a water-filled, continuous-duty submersible electric motor.

SECTION 2: OPERATING CONDITIONS & ENVIRONMENT
2.1 Liquid Pumped: Clear, cold drinking water, non-aggressive, free from abrasive solids.
2.2 Water Temperature: Ambient, not exceeding 33°C.
2.3 Installation Depth: Fully submerged up to a maximum water submergence depth of 15 meters below lowest water level.
2.4 Operating Environment: Open rural agricultural wells subject to seasonal water table drawdown.

SECTION 3: ELECTRICAL RATING & POWER SUPPLY
3.1 Power Rating: 7.5 kW (10.0 HP) continuous rated output.
3.2 Supply Voltage: 415 V, 3-Phase, 50 Hz A.C. supply.
3.3 Voltage Variation: Motor shall be capable of operating continuously within voltage band of 350V to 440V without winding overheating.
3.4 Motor Type: 2-Pole squirrel-cage induction type, water-lubricated thrust and journal bearings.

SECTION 4: GOVERNING STANDARDS (SPECIFIED CITATION)
4.1 The openwell submersible pumpsets shall strictly comply with the design, manufacturing, and performance requirements of IS 14220:1994 ("Openwell Submersible Pumpsets - Specification").
4.2 Testing of electrical insulation shall be carried out with 500V DC megger and must not be less than 5 Megaohms after 24 hours immersion.
4.3 Hydrostatic pressure testing of the pump casing shall be conducted at 1.5 times the maximum operating head.

SECTION 5: PERFORMANCE PARAMETERS & DUTY POINT
5.1 Rated Operating Head: 32.0 Meters (Total Dynamic Head).
5.2 Discharge at Duty Point: 18.0 Litres per Second (64.8 m³/hr).
5.3 Overall Efficiency: The overall efficiency (wire-to-water) of the complete pumpset at guaranteed duty point shall be not less than 48.0%.
5.4 Shut-off Head: Not less than 42.0 meters.

SECTION 6: INSTALLATION & MOUNTING PROVISIONS
6.1 The pumpset shall be designed for horizontal/vertical bed mounting at the bottom of the open well on a prefabricated civil pedestal.
6.2 Mechanical suspension eyes shall be provided on the motor casing to facilitate lowering and lifting via high-tensile stainless steel wire rope.
6.3 Flanged delivery connection of 80 mm nominal bore (NB) conforming to standard pipe flanging.
6.4 [INTENTIONALLY VAGUE REQUIREMENT]:
    "The entire assembly shall be manufactured with superior engineering workmanship, excellent corrosion resistance, and high-grade durable components to ensure long trouble-free service life under harsh village conditions."

SECTION 7: QUALITY ASSURANCE & INSPECTION
7.1 Manufacturer must furnish internal routine test certificates covering high-voltage testing, insulation resistance, and head-discharge-power characteristics before dispatch.
7.2 [INTENTIONALLY VAGUE REQUIREMENT]:
    "All pump materials shall be of standard commercial grade and suitable for water contact."
7.3 The departmental inspection officer reserves the right to witness prototype testing at the manufacturer's works.
