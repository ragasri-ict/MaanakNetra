Audit Verification Scope

  • Requirement Extraction Structured normalization of quantities, operating heads, power, and materials.
  • Indian Standards Matching Multi-signal hybrid retrieval matching against BIS standards dataset.
  • Lifecycle & Supersession Checks Flags obsolete revisions and provides active replacement references.
  • Statutory QCO Compliance Audits compliance against compulsory Scheme-I ISI Mark orders in Gazette of India.
  • Specification Linter Rules Flags vague subjective clauses ("superior workmanship", "standard grade").
  • Officer-Reviewable Corrections Side-by-side corrected specification ready for procurement officer sign-off.
Complies with General Financial Rules (GFR 2017) Rule 144 & BIS Act 2016.
